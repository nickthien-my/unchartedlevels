### Hunting Horn
#### HR7

- **Hidden Harmonic II** (Nargacuga branch) or **Gale Horn** combined with early-to-mid High Rank mixed armor.

- Alternatively, the **Rampage Agitato S** customized with high raw/white sharpness melodies if you have access to Rampage quests. [[1](https://gamestegy.com/post/monster-hunter-rise/490/hunting-horn-builds-progression-and-), [2](https://monsterhunterrise.wiki.fextralife.com/Hunting_Horn_Builds)]

Suggested HR 6 Armor Set & Pieces

- **Head:** Kaiser Crown or Channeler S (for Razor Sharp)

- **Chest:** Vaik Mail S or Narga Mail S

- **Arms:** Kaiser Vambraces or Lagombi S

- **Waist:** Anjanath Coil S (provides Attack Boost)

- **Legs:** Ingot Greaves S (great efficiency for Attack Boost and Critical Eye) [[1](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/boards/300976-monster-hunter-rise/79376813), [2](https://monsterhunterrise.wiki.fextralife.com/Hunting_Horn_Builds)]

Essential Skills to Prioritize

- **Horn Maestro Lv. 1:** Non-negotiable; speeds up song performance and boosts shockwave damage.

- **Attack Boost Lv. 4+:** Increases your flat raw damage.

- **Weakness Exploit Lv. 3:** Grants easy affinity when hitting monster weak spots (like the head).

- **Critical Eye / Critical Boost:** Scales up your physical damage output.

- **Speed Sharpening or Razor Sharp:** Keeps your sharpness high during long fights.

- **Slugger Lv. 2–3:** Optional comfort/utility skill to get more KO stun knockdowns when bonking the monster's head.
