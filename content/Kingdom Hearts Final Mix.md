## August 24th, 2026
Spend most of my gameplay time today grinding for the materials needed for synthesis. Normally, I would leave this grind towards the very end of the game. Which isn't a bad idea as you wouldn't sidetrack your progress and through the natural progress of the game you would have collected most of the necessary materials. The game doesn't put any progression necessary items in the synthesis shop besides some healing items and accessories until the very end, which is when you need those weapons.
The synthesis shop is gated mostly by the progress you've made in the game. The further you've progressed in the game and defeated the newer enemies then you'll have access to the materials necessary to build the more potent items and accessories. It's just that the available synthesis recipes require the players to complete the synthesis recipes of the lower tiers before unlocking the recipes of those in the higher tiers. So you can't jump straight to the ultimate weapon tier of recipes even if you have the necessary materials to craft them.
This creates a system that pretty much is meaningless if you wait til the end to engage with it because you would have the ability to unlock the higher level items in one swoop by the end and thus causing the crafted recipes in the lower tier redundant. Outside of being a checklist to tick off and a means to syphon off your materials away from the higher tier.
Because this has been my style of progression ever since I first played Kingdom Hearts all the way back in my teens. I decided to shake things up midway through my Proud playthrough as I wanted to see if the Synthesis system actually makes an impact on lowering the difficulty players face as they make their way through the game.
After making my way through most of the synthesis shop, I found that the items themselves don't play a huge role in making the game easier for a player that they should prioritize grinding the materials for them. Even recovery items like elixirs are more easily found as spoils from defeated enemies rather than spending the time to grind out the materials.
Thus the synthesis system itself seems to be a parallel progression that the players invest into from time to time and reap the reward at the end with the characters' ultimate weapons. But prior to that, one could look at the farming process for the progression of the synthesis shop as a chance to engage with the mechanics present in the game. Otherwise one could reasonable go through most of the game and not even engage with even the most basic mechanics like magic. So the hunt for the synthesis materials acts as a checklist for players to optionally engage with if they desire. Others may feel that the process is needlessly convoluted or mind-numbingly dull especially if the materials are from enemies that only need to be defeated to collect. I had the unfortunate luck experiencing this while farming for Lucid Crystals and spent nearly an hour going back and forth between rooms killing groups of that one specific enemies in the hopes that they will drop it.
The unintended consequence of farming synthesis materials as soon as they are available is that now I'm overleveled. Normally, when I played through the story I would quickly head to the next trigger point to start the next section of the story in each world. But now that I'm going back and forth triggering enemy encounters to get them to drop the wanted material. I'm at least 4-5 levels above what would be the sweet spot in terms of difficultly and challenge. This time around I'm mashing my way past the bosses in Hollow Bastion and defeating most enemies with one combo chain of attacks. This is prior to the upgraded enemies after completing Hollow Bastion, if I were to do the same amount of farming post-Hollow Bastion then I would be at a higher level compared to what I am at now due to enemies giving out at least double the experience points.
