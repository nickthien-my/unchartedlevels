### Recommended Structure

1. **Quick Cursory Intro:** Introduce the game and its historical context.
    
      
    
2. **Console Baseline Comparison:** Anchor your analysis to a widely accessible baseline (such as the standard PS4 version) to establish a universal control group for performance, resolution, and asset quality.
    
      
    
3. **Port Reality & Issues:** Identify out-of-the-box technical hurdles, engine limitations, and platform oversights (e.g., poor UI scaling, locked frame rates, or asset streaming bottlenecks).
    
      
    
4. **The Modernization Toolkit:** Provide your curated list of non-invasive, fidelity-focused fixes and mods (such as higher internal resolutions, engine stutter fixes, and UI scaling adjustments) that preserve developer intent while fixing platform blind spots.
    
      
    
5. **The Core Review:** Deliver the final critique of the game's design, narrative, and mechanics, unclouded by technical frustrations because the plumbing has already been solved.
