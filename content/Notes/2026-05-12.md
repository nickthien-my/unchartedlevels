## Metatextual Context in Games
It's been a while that I've listened to podcasts and one thing I heard today while listening to an August 2025 episode of [[Rebel FM]] was Arthur Gies talking about [[Metal Gear Solid Delta - Snake Eater]] and how compared to [[Metal Gear Solid]] and [[Metal Gear Solid 2 - Sons of Liberty]] Metal Gear Solid Delta's story did not feel like [[Hideo Kojima]] had much to say. That the story felt more like Kojima throwing things out just to find something about the game that he could be excited about.
I don't think there are a lot of games that we can attribute all decisions to a single director, but the early Metal Gear games and perhaps the [[Death Stranding]] franchises are those that one could look into it.
