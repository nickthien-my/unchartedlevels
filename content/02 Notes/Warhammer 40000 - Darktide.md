### Notes
#### 7th June, 2026
First off, Darktide does not have cross-save support. Similarly to games like [[Helldivers 2]], all your progress is stuck on the system that you played on. I had nearly leveled four operators to level 30 on the [[PC]] version and now I'm starting from scratch on the [[PlayStation 5]] version. Which is kind of disappointing but it has cropped up some unintended consequences that has made my trophy hunting progress on the PlayStation 5 much easier.
